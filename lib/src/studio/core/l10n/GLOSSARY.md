# Studio glossary (one term per thing, used everywhere, no synonyms)

Arabic is modern Egyptian, short and direct, written the way a colleague speaks.
English words the team says at work stay English or in Arabic script as listed.
Platform and channel names stay in English script: Instagram, TikTok, Reel, Story, Feed.
Brand and product words stay in English: Jarz, Studio, flavor, size, texture, filling, dessert, order, costly, Cheesecake.
Numbers are digits. Buttons are an imperative of one or two words. No «يرجى», «الرجاء», «تم بنجاح», «قم بـ», «عملية», «إجراء».
Address is gender-neutral where possible (imperatives, «ممكن», «لازم», passives like «اتحفظ»).
Roles go by role, never by a person's name.

## English style

Plain words, sentence case, no exclamation marks, no «successfully», no «please».
Every empty state says what to do first. Every error says what happened and what to do next.
A status says what happens next and who has it, by role.

- who posts a post (post screen line): By hand (Reem) / هتنزلها: يدوي (Reem); The system / هتنزل: بالسيستم

- Page not found / الصفحة مش موجودة; Back to Today / ارجع للنهارده
- OK / Cancel on every date and time picker: تمام / إلغاء (one helper, shared/studio_pickers.dart)
- Required input: label ends with " *" and the line "Required" / «مطلوب»; a required date reads "(required)" / «(مطلوب)»
